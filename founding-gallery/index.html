MEMBERSHIP, BY INTRODUCTION

100 spots.
One could be yours.

Good judgement. Things made possible. Someone who follows through.

An all-star EA to organise your life. A concierge to make things happen. A coach in your corner. That’s your zuzu.

We’re welcoming 100 founding members by personal invitation. Each gets their own numbered card and an individual zuzu emblem. Only 100 of these editions will ever exist.

How do I become a member?

Every invitation starts with someone on the zuzu team. Each can invite up to five people. If you know someone building zuzu, ask them for an introduction.

What makes my edition mine?

Your number, your individual zuzu, and the name of the person who invited you. One edition for each of the first 100 members.
